# Community Behavior

[简体中文](/docs/engineering/website/code_of_conduct/) · **English**


Respect participants with different experiences and backgrounds. Discussions should focus on reproducible facts and technical issues; harassment, discrimination, threats, unauthorized disclosure of personal privacy, or malicious interference are not permitted. Maintainers may remove violating content and restrict participation. For questions involving privacy or security, use the private reporting channel specified in SECURITY.md.
