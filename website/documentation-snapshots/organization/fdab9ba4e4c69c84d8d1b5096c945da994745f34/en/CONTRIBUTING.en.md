# Contributing to Weapp Container

[简体中文](CONTRIBUTING.md) · **English**

Follow the pinned tools, source inventory, and test commands in each repository's README. Reproduce the problem first, then describe the behavior being changed, validation scope, and limitations. Platform support claims require independent evidence tied to the relevant version.

Do not commit credentials, signing material, business data, real device identifiers, local paths, raw device logs, or private release attachments. Preserve third-party licenses and provenance; do not overwrite historical tags, assets, or hashes. SDK changes must refresh SOURCE-INVENTORY while retaining ORIGIN as the original extraction record.

Contributions use the applicable license of the target repository. Contributors must have the right to contribute their content.
