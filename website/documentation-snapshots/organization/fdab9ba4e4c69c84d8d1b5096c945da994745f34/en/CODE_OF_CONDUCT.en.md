# Community code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · **English**

Respect contributors with different backgrounds and experience. Keep discussion focused on reproducible facts and code. Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, and persistent disruption are prohibited. Maintainers may remove violating content or restrict participation and should explain their decision.

For privacy and security reports, use the private channels in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.en.md).
